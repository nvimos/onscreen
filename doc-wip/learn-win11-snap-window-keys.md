Q: what are the keys to snap windows in win11 to borders?

Win+Left or Win+Right: snap to left or right half of the screen.
Win+Up: maximize, or move a snapped window to the top half.
Win+Down: restore, or move a snapped window to the bottom half.
Win+Left then Win+Up (or Down): snap to a quarter (top-left, bottom-left, etc.). Repeat the other arrow for the opposite side.
Win+Z: open the Snap Layouts picker to choose a layout.
Win+Shift+Left or Right: move the window to the next monitor.
Win+Shift+Up or Down: stretch the window to full height, or restore its height.

Snap Layouts and Snap Assist must be enabled in Settings > System > Multitasking if these keys do nothing.
