1. FIVE WAYS TO WELLBEING				*you-wellbeing*

From the New Economics Foundation (2008). Small daily actions.

  Connect		Talk to people: family, friends, colleagues,
			neighbours. Call instead of texting.
  Be active		Walk, cycle, play, dance. Find something you enjoy.
  Take notice		Look around. Notice the weather, the seasons, how
			you feel. Look up from the screen.
  Keep learning		Try something new, pick up an old interest, learn
			to fix or cook something.
  Give			Do something for someone else. Say thank you,
			help out, volunteer.

2. HALT							*you-halt*

From the 12-step programs. Before acting on a craving, a bad mood or a
rash decision, ask: am I

  H  Hungry		Eat a proper meal.
  A  Angry		Name it, walk it off, talk to someone.
  L  Lonely		Reach out. Call someone, go where people are.
  T  Tired		Rest, nap, go to bed.

Fix the basic need first. The problem often shrinks after that.

3. MINIMUM PHYSICAL ACTIVITY				*you-activity*

Helsedirektoratet (Norwegian Directorate of Health), condensed.

Adults ~
  - 150-300 minutes of moderate activity a week, or 75-150 minutes of
    vigorous activity, or a mix.
  - Muscle strengthening for the large muscle groups, 2+ days a week.
  - Sit less. Break up long sitting. Any activity beats none.

Older adults (65+) ~
  - As adults, plus balance training 3+ days a week.

Children and youth (6-17) ~
  - 60+ minutes of moderate to vigorous activity every day.
  - Vigorous activity and strengthening 3+ days a week.

Moderate: faster breathing, can still talk (brisk walk).
Vigorous: hard breathing, talking is hard (running).

4. HOW MUCH ALCOHOL IS SAFE				*you-alcohol*

No amount is risk free. Less is better, none is best.

  - "A glass a day is healthy" was a study bias: ill ex-drinkers were
    counted as abstainers. Corrected, light drinkers live no longer.
  - Death risk rises clearly from ~25 g/day (women), ~45 g/day (men).
    One drink is 10-14 g.
  - Under 40 years of age, the lowest-risk amount is zero.
  - Alcohol causes at least 7 cancers, from the first drink.
  - Nordic Nutrition Recommendations 2023 (Helsedirektoratet): no safe
    limit.

Papers ~
  Zhao et al. JAMA Netw Open. 2023;6(3):e236185.
  doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.6185

  GBD 2020 Alcohol Collaborators. Lancet. 2022;400(10347):185-235.
